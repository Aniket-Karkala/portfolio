I'm Aniket, a robotics engineer and mentor at Innovation Story in Bandra, Mumbai.

I design embedded and robotics hardware and mentor school students through engineering projects for science fairs and robotics competitions. Most of my builds start with a sensor and a question, run on ESP32 or Arduino boards, and end up in a 3D-printed enclosure on a test bench.

I trained as an electronics and telecommunication engineer with a minor in IoT, and spent my internships at Reliance Jio working on network security and cloud. That background shows up in how I debug.
