Sensors in, something useful out.

I work at Innovation Story in Bandra, where I design embedded and robotics hardware and mentor students from Grade 4 upward through real engineering projects.

Most of what I build starts with a sensor and a question. Can a cane feel a pothole before a foot does? Can two cheap gas sensors tell a naturally ripened banana from a chemically ripened one? Can a camera decide when a cake is done? I prototype on ESP32 and Arduino boards, print the mechanical parts, and test on the bench until the numbers hold up.
