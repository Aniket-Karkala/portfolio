Water quality monitor

A battery-powered probe set for turbidity, pH, dissolved solids and temperature.

The problem.

Cheap water sensors are easy to buy and easy to wire wrongly. Most output more voltage than an ESP32 pin can survive.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • Turbidity sensor
  • pH sensor
  • TDS sensor
  • DS18B20 temperature probe

Core

  • ESP32 DevKit
  • Temperature compensation

Outputs

  • NeoPixel status LED
  • 18650 cell with 5 V boost

How it works.

  1. Turbidity and pH sensors feed the ESP32 through resistor dividers.
  2. A dissolved-solids sensor and a waterproof temperature probe work together, so the reading is corrected for temperature.
  3. A single lithium cell is boosted to 5 V for the sensors.
  4. One status LED shows the state at a glance.

What I learned building it.

  • All analog sensors sit on ADC1, because ADC2 stops working when Wi-Fi is on.
  • The firmware multiplies each divided reading by 1.5 to recover the true sensor voltage.
