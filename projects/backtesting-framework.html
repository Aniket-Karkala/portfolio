Testing a trading strategy against prop-firm rules before risking the fee.

The problem.

A prop-firm challenge is a rule set as much as a market: daily loss limits, maximum drawdown, profit target. A strategy has to survive all of them at once.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • Price data
  • Challenge rules

Core

  • Python
  • Strategy and risk modules
  • Walk-forward validation

Outputs

  • Pass or fail verdict
  • Equity and drawdown charts

How it works.

  1. Seven modules cover data, strategy, risk, rule enforcement, the engine and reporting.
  2. The strategy is an EMA crossover with ATR-based stops across forex, indices and gold.
  3. A challenge simulator enforces a 5% daily loss limit, 10% maximum drawdown, a 10% target and four minimum trading days.
  4. Walk-forward validation tunes on the older half of the data, locks the parameters, and tests on the newer half.

What I learned building it.

  • Walk-forward testing caught a textbook overfit: the optimiser's best setting had been chosen on only two trades.
  • Risk is fixed at 0.5% of the account per trade.
