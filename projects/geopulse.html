GeoPulse

A live estimate of India's oil price next to a measure of world tension.

Research paperMid-size projectResearch
InputsMarket dataNews feeds
CorePython and FlaskNowcast modelInstability index
OutputsWeb dashboardResearch paper

The problem.

Official figures for India's crude oil basket arrive late. A nowcast estimates the present from data that is already available.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • Market data
  • News feeds

Core

  • Python and Flask
  • Nowcast model
  • Instability index

Outputs

  • Web dashboard
  • Research paper

How it works.

  1. A web app nowcasts the price of India's crude oil basket.
  2. A geopolitical instability index is built from news.
  3. The two are shown together and written up as a research paper.
