Wireless flight-sim controller

A handheld controller and USB dongle from two ESP32-S3 boards.

The problem.

Commercial handheld flight controllers cost around $150. The same idea can be built from common parts.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • Hall-effect thumbstick
  • AS5600 rudder encoder
  • Slide potentiometers

Core

  • ESP32-S3 handheld
  • ESP32-S3 dongle
  • ESP-NOW link

Outputs

  • USB joystick

How it works.

  1. One ESP32-S3 in the handheld reads sticks, sliders and switches.
  2. It sends small packets over ESP-NOW to a second ESP32-S3 plugged into the computer.
  3. The dongle appears as a standard USB joystick, so no drivers are needed.

What I learned building it.

  • This one is still on paper. The mechanical parts, the rudder linkage and slider detents, are the hard part.
