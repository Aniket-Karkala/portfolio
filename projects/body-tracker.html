Four-IMU body tracker

Four motion sensors on a dancer, checked against a webcam.

The problem.

Is a camera good enough to analyse a pirouette, or do you need sensors on the body?

What is inside.

Inputs

  • 4× BNO085 IMU
  • Webcam

Core

  • ESP32, shared SPI bus
  • YOLO-Pose pipeline

Outputs

  • Wi-Fi quaternion stream
  • Side-by-side comparison

How it works.

  1. Four IMUs sit on the left and right shin, the spine and the head.
  2. All four share one SPI bus on an ESP32 and stream orientation as quaternions over Wi-Fi.
  3. The same movement is captured by a webcam and run through a pose-estimation model.
  4. The two pipelines are compared.

What I learned building it.

  • Getting four sensors to start reliably on one bus is the hard part and is still being worked on.
