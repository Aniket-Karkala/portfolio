Smart Temple

A model temple that manages its own crowd, built for the World Robot Olympiad.

Mentored buildLarge projectRobotics and play

The problem.

Crowded temples need to know how many people are in each area and when to hold the gates. This model does it automatically.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • 9× PN532 RFID readers
  • RFID visitor cards

Core

  • ESP32, dual-core tasks
  • Zone capacity logic

Outputs

  • Servo gates via PCA9685
  • Dispenser motors via L298N

How it works.

  1. Visitors carry RFID cards, and nine readers on one SPI bus track which zone each card is in.
  2. Each zone has a visitor limit. Servo gates open and close to hold the count.
  3. DC motors dispense prasadam and vibhuti.
  4. The ESP32 splits Wi-Fi and control work across its two cores.

What I learned building it.

  • I wrote two handbooks so Grade 4 and 5 students could follow the whole build: a 20-chapter general volume and a 25-page deep-dive that uses real values from the firmware.
  • Two characters, Robo and Guru Owl, carry the story through every chapter.
