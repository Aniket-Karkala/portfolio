OvenEye

A clip-on camera that tells you when the bake is done.

The problem.

Opening the oven door to check a bake drops the temperature, and timers do not know what the food looks like.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • OV2640 camera
  • White reference tile

Core

  • XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense
  • Browning index
  • ETA regression

Outputs

  • Self-hosted Wi-Fi dashboard
  • Time-to-done estimate

How it works.

  1. A camera pod hangs from the oven door handle and looks through the window.
  2. A browning index is computed from pixel brightness, normalised against a white ceramic tile placed inside the oven.
  3. A straight-line fit over the last six frames predicts the time left to the target colour.
  4. The board serves its own Wi-Fi dashboard with snapshots and live readings.

What I learned building it.

  • No machine learning is involved. The index is arithmetic and the estimate is a least-squares fit.
  • The mount is a three-part PETG pendant: a clamp on the handle, an arm, and the camera pod. Nothing rests on the glass.
  • A soft lip on the shroud blocks room reflections, with a vent notch so heat cannot build up.
  • The parts list comes to about ₹2,900.
