Folding-mast sensor head

A robot mast that folds, with its sensors at the tip.

Own buildMid-size projectRobotics and play
InputsVL53L1X ToFLidar
CoreESP32-C3 at the tipMain controller at the base
OutputsFour-contact pogo jointChecksummed serial link

The problem.

When a mast folds, wires through the hinge fatigue and break. Spring contacts that only close when the mast is up avoid that, but ten contacts are hard to align.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • VL53L1X ToF
  • Lidar

Core

  • ESP32-C3 at the tip
  • Main controller at the base

Outputs

  • Four-contact pogo joint
  • Checksummed serial link

How it works.

  1. A time-of-flight sensor and a lidar sit at the tip of the mast.
  2. A small ESP32-C3 at the tip reads them and sends checksummed serial packets down.
  3. That cuts the joint from ten contacts to four: power, ground and two data lines.
  4. Pogo pins close the circuit only when the mast is fully deployed.

What I learned building it.

  • A hinge drags pins sideways as it closes, which is the failure mode to design around.
  • Contact debouncing and switching the load on after the pins seat prevent arcing.
