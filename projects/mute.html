The problem.

For people with misophonia, ordinary sounds such as chewing can be unbearable. Blocking all sound is not a way to live.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • Audio

Core

  • Trigger-sound detection

Outputs

How it works.

  1. Trigger sounds are detected in the audio stream.
  2. Only those sounds are suppressed, and the rest of the soundscape is left alone.
