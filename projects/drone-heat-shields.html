Heat shields for firefighting drones

How to keep a multirotor alive near a fire.

Research paperMid-size projectResearch
Inputs28 published sourcesMaterial property data
CoreHeat-transfer analysisFigure of merit Φ = 1/(kρ)
OutputsFive-page paperRanked materials

The problem.

A drone that flies close to a fire is limited by how fast heat reaches its frame, motors and battery.

What is inside.

Inputs

  • 28 published sources
  • Material property data

Core

  • Heat-transfer analysis
  • Figure of merit Φ = 1/(kρ)

Outputs

  • Five-page paper
  • Ranked materials

How it works.

  1. The paper draws on 28 published sources on plastics, aerogels, coatings and heat buffers.
  2. It proposes a mass-specific figure of merit, Φ = 1/(kρ), that ranks insulation regardless of thickness.
  3. A worked sizing case shows how to use it.

What I learned building it.

  • A sign error in the absorbed-flux equation was found and corrected during revision.
  • A twelve-page manuscript was compressed to five pages with a 241-word abstract.
